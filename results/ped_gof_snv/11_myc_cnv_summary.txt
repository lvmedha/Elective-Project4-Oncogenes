  is being driven by amplification, not the residue change. The
  AM-likely-benign call is structurally correct (those residues are
  not destabilising) but biologically uninformative for an oncogene
  whose activation mechanism is gene dosage / overexpression.
  Burkitt-style B-cell lymphoma lines may have near-diploid CN but
  high MYC mRNA via IG-MYC translocation -- CN alone cannot detect
  that case (mRNA analysis would).
