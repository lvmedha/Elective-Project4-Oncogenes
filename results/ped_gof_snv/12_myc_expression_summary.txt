Interpretation:
  Expression closes the gap that CN alone leaves open. Burkitt-style
  B-cell lymphoma lines (e.g. RAJI) carry IG-MYC translocations:
  near-diploid CN but highly elevated MYC mRNA. If the puzzling
  AM-likely-benign lines sit at or above the top-quartile MYC
  expression threshold, their dependency is explained by
  overexpression, not the residue change. AM scores the residue,
  so the likely-benign call is structurally correct but biologically
  uninformative for an oncogene whose activation mechanism is dosage.
